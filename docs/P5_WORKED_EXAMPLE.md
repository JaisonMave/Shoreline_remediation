## How to read this

- The rules and the field evidence answer different questions: the rules screen what is operationally and ecologically acceptable; the evidence says what happened to edge erosion and vegetation where each method was used. Where they disagree, both are shown and neither overrides the other.
- All evidence comes from one spill (Deepwater Horizon), one oil and one marsh type. Associations, not proven causes, except the planting contrast, which was randomized.
- The tool predicts physical (edge loss) and biological (vegetation) recovery; it does not predict chemical recovery (no soil or sediment chemistry in the model; see REVIEW_NOTES E0).
